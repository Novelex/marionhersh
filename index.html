New research · 2026

Older Autistic Adults’ Experiences, Attitudes and Support Needs

New empirical data on the living and employment situations, aspirations and barriers of older autistic people, giving them a voice through their own words. Appropriate support emerges as a key enabler of quality of life, but more often its absence acts as a barrier.

Read the full paper

  • 78 participants autistic people aged 50 to 79
  • Co-produced by a team of older autistic researchers and an advisory board
  • Funded by UKRI Healthy Ageing Catalyst Award
