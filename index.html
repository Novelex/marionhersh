Using technology to overcome the barriers experienced by disabled and older people.

Marion Hersh is a Senior Lecturer in Biomedical Engineering at the University of Glasgow. They believe in working to make the world a better place for people, animals and the planet itself, and that research can be used to make positive change as well as contribute to knowledge.

They increasingly use co-production approaches to research, to empower the groups being researched.

Research

  • Older autistic people

    Living situations, employment and technology use, and technology development to support autonomy and quality of life.

  • Mobility for blind people

    Route learning, spatial understanding and representations, and new travel technologies.

  • Accessible learning technologies

    Designing learning technologies that include disabled learners.

  • Engineering ethics

    Opening up engineering to minority groups, and the experiences of disabled and LGBTQ+ engineers.
