UCU Scotland Green Network

The UCU Scotland Green Network has now had two meetings, attended by several different branches. Among other things, we are working on a questionnaire for branches to send to management, to investigate the progress they are making on various sustainability issues.

Future meetings and events are open to all UCU Scotland members.

Solidarity to branches taking action or balloting

Solidarity with all UCU branches taking industrial action against job losses and for job security, as well as to save Scholar and on other issues. Good luck to branches engaged in balloting.

Solidarity to UCU Unite and their industrial action.

UCU NEC elections 2026

The election ran from 30 January to 2 March 2026.

We need NEC members who will support branches in dispute and take action to end the threat to jobs, including through casualisation, as well as action to end excessive workload. We also need strong action in support of climate justice, wider solidarity nationally and internationally, and on equality issues, and against war, militarism and discrimination. I am asking UCU members to vote for me, for Sean and Regi as HE and FE VPs, and for the other UCU Left candidates.
